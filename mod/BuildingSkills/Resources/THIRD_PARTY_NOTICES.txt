templates. SML is installed separately by Satisfactory Mod Manager; this package
does not distribute a modified SML runtime. SML's upstream license is GPLv3;
its license text is included as SML_LICENSE.txt for reference. The Building
Skills MIT notice does not replace the licenses of dependencies.
