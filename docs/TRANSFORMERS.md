# Transformers

In normal Edit, choose an anchor with Alt+A, then press **Alt+X** (rebindable
**Add Transformer at Anchor**). The transformer keeps that anchor's position and
orientation at creation; it does not follow subsequent anchor movement. Anchor
scale is ignored. The shortcut is only active when an anchor exists.

Each transformer has editable position (cm) and rotation (degrees: X roll,
Y pitch, Z yaw). Coordinates are world-based unless **Original anchor** is checked;
then they are relative to the anchor position and orientation captured at creation.
Changing that checkbox changes only the coordinate display, not the transformer's
placement. Editing takes effect with Apply; Cancel discards the draft.

**K** opens Edit settings, including an ordered transformer list. Select an entry,
move it Up/Down, remove it, or change its settings, then Apply. Connected and Volume
settings remain accessible from this window when those modes are selected.
Transform's step window also has a Transformers button. Build mode's K binding is
unchanged.

**Build as blueprint** is one shared, session-only option for the player's entire
list, initially off. Each construction action combines its original buildings and
all successful transformer copies into one native blueprint selection. It also
works with an empty list or a placement outside every transformer's radius.
Existing blueprint membership is replaced for those buildings; nested Building
Skills groups are preserved. This creates a placed blueprint identity, not a saved
blueprint file. Construction undo still removes the complete action together.

- **Mirror** is enabled initially and uses the transformer's local X/Z plane.
- **Rotation** rotates one copy by the entered angle around local Z. Enabling either
  mode disables the other. Disabling both leaves the transformer inactive.
- **Effect radius** is measured in meters from the transformer origin, default 64 m.
  The whole construction action qualifies by its bounding-box center. Blueprints
  and batches are copied as whole placements, rather than filtering their members.
- **Color** controls the translucent mirror plane or rotation beam. Use the color
  wheel and brightness control.
- **Repeat player** defaults on and repeats the player's original construction.
- **Repeat earlier transformers** defaults off. When enabled, it also repeats
  copies generated by entries above it, if their placement centers are in range.

Every entry runs once, top to bottom. An entry cannot repeat its own output or the
output of a later entry. Multiple applicable entries produce multiple copies.
The session limit is 16 transformers; one construction action can generate at most
64 copies and 2048 extra buildings. Reaching a limit reports a message.

While holding a Build hologram, generated holograms follow the same ordered
geometry and radius rules. These are visual previews, not extra construction
inputs. Copies use building archives with internal references remapped to their
new buildings. Each copy requires its normal construction materials, unless No
Build Cost is active. Insufficient materials skip that copy and its descendants;
the original placement remains. Copies join the original construction's undo entry.
An exact overlap with an existing lightweight building skips the whole copy with
a message, preserving unambiguous selection and undo. This includes a foundation
whose position and rotation would be unchanged by the mirror.

When a cable is placed between buildings that were repeated earlier, its copy
connects to the corresponding repeated buildings. Missing, ambiguous or full
endpoints skip the cable with a message; copies never connect back to the source
circuit. Connections contained within a copied batch remain inside that batch.

Mirror keeps the game's right-handed orientation and the original positive scale.
Positions are reflected through the plane. Machines choose their orientation from
their belt and pipe port layout, matching inputs with inputs and outputs with
outputs, while keeping the reflected up direction. A constructor whose output
faces the mirror therefore has a counterpart whose output also faces the mirror.
Holograms and subsequent matching actions use the same orientation rule.

This preserves the original mesh's handedness; it does not create reversed
versions of asymmetric building models. Exact reflection of every port is not
always possible on an asymmetric machine. The orientation prioritizes port roles
and directions; copied flexible connections fit the actual copied ports. Spline
point positions and tangents are reflected as well.
When splitter outlets exchange sides, copied connections and smart-splitter
sorting rules follow the corresponding mirrored outlet. Original buildings and
their connections are unchanged. Existing copies made with an older orientation
rule are not rotated automatically; re-create an incorrectly facing copy.

Transformers also repeat **dismantling, materials and swatches** onto existing
matching buildings. The same ordering, radius, Repeat player and Repeat earlier
transformers settings apply. A candidate must have the same structural building
type, position, rotation and scale; current material and swatch are ignored.
Material variants of the same foundation count as the same structural type.
Spline geometry, cable endpoints and conveyor lift height must also match.
Connected cables match corresponding named sockets on transformed buildings;
those sockets can be offset from a mathematically reflected point because the
machines retain their original mesh handedness.
Missing counterparts are skipped; ambiguous overlapping counterparts are skipped
with a message rather than choosing one arbitrarily.

Dismantling resolves counterparts before removing anything, then uses one native
deletion action. One undo restores that complete action. Material and swatch
painting works with both Default and Connected spread: the Customizer previews
matching counterparts, then applies the native customization to eligible targets,
including its normal costs. Matching happens before material replacements or
swatch changes, so the operation cannot change its own target search.
Changing a lightweight building's material preserves its existing swatch and
custom colors, even when the replacement material has a different default swatch.

Lists belong to individual players and last only for their current session. They
are not written into world saves. Built copies are ordinary saved buildings.

Transformers also repeat **translation, rotation and scaling** performed in Edit's
Transform state. Counterparts are matched against the buildings' poses before
the action; the resulting poses follow the same mirror or rotation rule as
construction. The same radius, ordering and repeat checkboxes apply. Matching
does not create missing buildings or add counterparts to your selection.

Selected buildings and matching counterparts move in one validated transaction.
Connected pipes, belts and cables are planned together, so connections between
moving and stationary buildings are adjusted once. Undo/redo records the complete
action, including connected geometry, and does not search for new counterparts.
Buildings explicitly included in the selection receive the requested transform
once. If different transformer paths demand incompatible moves for the same
unselected building, the action is rejected before anything moves.
