# Short gameplay clips

Record the first four clips for the initial mod page. The last two are optional
follow-ups. Aim for 15–30 seconds per clip, with one feature and a clearly visible
result. Use the current 0.10.9 build and default bindings, or show your rebound
keys in the captions.

## Recording setup

- Use a small demonstration factory in daylight, with a clear background and no
  personal names or multiplayer chat on screen.
- Record at 1920×1080, 30 or 60 fps, landscape. Keep the native tool hints visible.
- Use game audio or silent footage; narration is optional.
- Start and finish with a two-second pause so clips are easy to trim and loop.
- Prefer one continuous take. Remove menu navigation and loading screens.
- Keep the original recordings; provide MP4 files named as below.

## 01 — Select, group and move

**File:** `01-group-and-transform.mp4`

**Caption:** “Select once. Move the whole group.”

Prepare four foundations and a constructor.

1. Open Edit with **Alt+E**.
2. Hold **Ctrl** and sweep across the foundations and constructor. Pause on the
   green selection so the viewer sees what will move.
3. Press **G** to group, then **T** to enter Transform.
4. Nudge the group twice horizontally, then once vertically.
5. Use **Z** to undo the last transform and pause on the result.

Frame the original location and destination together. Use a small step so the
selection stays in view. Do not include several different rotation/scale modes
in this clip.

## 02 — Copy a selection

**File:** `02-copy-selection.mp4`

**Caption:** “Copy a layout without making a blueprint first.”

Prepare a short, recognizable row of two machines with a belt between them.

1. Open Edit and multi-select the complete little layout.
2. Press **Alt+C**. Pause on the placement hologram.
3. Move the hologram beside the original and place it.
4. Step back to show both layouts.

If desired, record a separate 10-second take showing **Alt+S** opening the
blueprint details window. Avoid exposing a long list of personal blueprints.

## 03 — Mirror construction

**File:** `03-mirror-transformer.mp4`

**Caption:** “Build once. Repeat across a mirror plane.”

Set up one mirror transformer before recording: in Edit choose an anchor with
**Alt+A**, add it with **Alt+X**, and use **K** to leave Mirror and Repeat player
enabled. Position the plane so the copy will be clearly separated.

1. Hold a constructor hologram on one side of the visible mirror plane.
2. Move it slowly so both the original and repeated previews are visible.
3. Place the constructor.
4. Show both constructors, especially their output directions.
5. Optionally undo the construction once to show the complete action disappearing.

Use only one transformer for this take. The preview and result should be readable
without opening its settings window.

## 04 — Plan parallel rails

**File:** `04-rail-planner.mp4`

**Caption:** “Plan the route. Build connected tracks.”

Prepare an open, flat demonstration area and one personal plan. Enable No Build
Cost in this demonstration world or carry the needed materials.

1. Open Plan with **Alt+P** and place three nodes making a gentle curve.
2. Open **K** and add one parallel at a clear sideways offset, for example local
   Y = 8 m. Keep the X/Z offsets at zero for this simple demonstration.
3. Close K and pause on the two planned paths.
4. Open K, use **Build full plan**, then close Plan.
5. Pause on the constructed rails.

Keep the route short and free of red invalid sections. If this needs more than
30 seconds, split it into “Plan and edit” and “Build the plan.” Do not speed up
footage so much that the controls become unreadable.

## 05 — Connected painting (optional)

**File:** `05-connected-painting.mp4`

**Caption:** “Preview and paint connected buildings together.”

Prepare a connected patch of foundations, with a separate patch beyond the gap.

1. Hold a contrasting swatch in the Customizer.
2. Press **R** to show **Spread: Connected** beneath the cursor.
3. Aim at the connected patch and pause on the multi-building preview.
4. Apply the swatch and move the cursor away to reveal the result.

Keep the separate patch in frame to make the selection boundary clear.

## 06 — Stick placement (optional)

**File:** `06-stick-placement.mp4`

**Caption:** “Reach farther with connected spline segments.”

Use a supported spline type in a clear area. For the easiest visual, show a
gently curving rail route.

1. Begin a rail and press **Alt+S** to enable Stick.
2. Scroll to extend the endpoint; keep the distance indication visible.
3. Adjust the endpoint slightly with **Alt+scroll** or **Ctrl+scroll**.
4. Place the route, then show its connected segments.

Choose a route you have checked before recording. This is a demonstration,
not the place to showcase an unresolved placement failure.

## Delivery and page placement

Put recordings under `Saved/Publication/0.10.9/videos/`. These videos are page
media and must not be inserted into the mod's runtime ZIP.

The listing can feature the first two clips near “Start here,” the mirror clip
under “Repeat with transformers,” and the rail clip under “Plan rail networks.”
Use the site's supported media upload/embed controls, or an unlisted video host
link after choosing where to publish. No hosting account or external upload has
been created.

Before publishing, watch each complete clip and check that the shown result
matches the caption, no private information is visible, and the UI text is
readable at the size used on the page.
